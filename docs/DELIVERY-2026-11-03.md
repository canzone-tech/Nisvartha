## 3. Blockers before public production launch

1. **Merge/release authority:** review the consolidated PR diff, migration chain `0001`–`0009` (append only), and release notes. Obtain explicit approval to merge/default branch and deploy; no agent should silently do either.
2. **Official content/identity:** authorized Foundation owner approves legal name/registration facts, mission/copy, contact email/phone/address, brand logo, evidence for impact metrics, photos/consent and published activities/projects. Do not invent or migrate unlicensed copy from reference sites.
3. **Privacy and operating policy:** approve published privacy notice, website terms, consent language and versions, data-controller contact, retention schedule and staff processing rules. Until then public forms and member registration remain disabled.
4. **Infrastructure:** select an authorized production host, DNS/domain/TLS access and environment secrets; securely provision MySQL 8.4 with least privilege; configure internal API URL, private media persistence and actual ClamAV scanner. `infra/compose.verify.yml` is **test-only** and is NOT a production deployment stack. There is no verified production deployment artifact/process in the reviewed repository.
5. **Production recovery and security:** TLS/HSTS, restricted ports/firewall, protected staff bootstrap, strong credentials/MFA decision, request logs/monitoring, encrypted backup of DB **and** private media, isolated restore drill, rollback procedure, patching and owner escalation process.
6. **Acceptance / UAT:** on actual staging/production-equivalent target, check approved routes and published content, forms **only after privacy approval**, staff authorization/publication flow, image approval/rights, keyboard and screen-reader navigation, cross-browser/mobile, performance and health/readiness. Record defects, screenshot evidence and designated sign-off.
7. **Financial scope remains excluded:** the stakeholder has confirmed only **one proposed payment at registration**, never installments. This does not establish legal permissibility, approved amount, settled payment proof or 1:3 compensation legality; it does not unlock live paid issuance or recruiting-based returns.
